About Us

Tools Website exists for one frustrating moment: you are filling an exam or job application form, everything is ready — and the form rejects your photo because it is 3 KB too big.

What we build

We build free online tools that produce an exact, downloadable file: a photo compressed to precisely 50 KB, a signature at exactly 140×60 pixels, a passport photo on a white background, a set of documents merged into one PDF under the form's limit. Most tools on the internet give you “smaller”; ours give you the exact size the form demands, with presets for popular exams and employers so you do not have to memorise specifications.

Why browser-based?

Every tool here runs entirely in your own browser. Your photo, signature and documents are never uploaded to a server — there is no server. That means:

  • Privacy: your personal documents stay on your device.
  • Speed: no uploading or downloading queues — results are instant.
  • Free forever: with no server costs to cover, we can keep every tool free, with no sign-up and no daily limits.

Who it is for

Students and job applicants filling forms for exams and recruiters (FPSC, PPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET and many more), Urdu speakers who are usually ignored by big tools sites, and creators who need exact platform sizes for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. The site is designed mobile-first, because for most of our visitors the phone is the computer.

How the site is supported

The site shows advertising (Google AdSense) so the tools can remain free for everyone. Advertising never changes a tool's result, and no tool ever asks for payment to download a file.
