Image & Form Tools

Online application forms are strict: a photo must be an exact number of kilobytes, an exact pixel size, and usually a JPG. These tools do that fitting for you — pick a preset for your exam (FPSC, PPSC, SSC, IBPS, NEET and more) or type the exact size you need, and download a file the form will accept. Everything runs in your browser, so your photo never leaves your phone.
