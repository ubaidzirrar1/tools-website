Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is completely free, with no sign-up, no account and no daily limits. The site is supported by advertising so the tools can stay free for students and job applicants.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your own browser using JavaScript. Your photo, signature or document never leaves your device — which also means the tools work instantly, even on slow connections.

Why does an online form reject my photo?

Usually it is a size problem: the file is larger than the allowed KB limit, the pixel dimensions do not match the specification, or the format is wrong (for example PNG instead of JPG). Our form tools set the exact KB, pixels and format the form asks for, so the upload is accepted.

What sizes do exam and job forms usually need?

It varies by exam. Common examples: SSC and IBPS photos are typically 20–50 KB at 200×230 pixels and signatures 10–20 KB, while many Pakistani exams such as FPSC set their own KB limit at a fixed size. Each tool has preset buttons for popular exams so you do not have to remember the numbers.

Do the tools work on a mobile phone?

Yes. The site is designed mobile-first and every tool runs in a phone browser — no app install needed. Take a photo, resize it to the exact size your form needs, and download the finished file in under a minute.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts on this site. Open a tool, add your file, and download the result — that is all.
