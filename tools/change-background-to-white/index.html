Change Background to White

Most ID and form photos demand a plain white background — but most of us take photos in a bedroom, not a studio. This tool removes whatever is behind you and replaces it with clean white.

Launching with the MVP build: this tool is in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly.

How it will work

  1. Upload a front-facing photo — any background is fine.
  2. The person is separated from the background automatically, in your browser.
  3. Download the photo on a pure white background, in the size and KB your form needs.

For the cleanest result

  • Take the photo where your outline is clear — avoid hair blending into a dark background.
  • Good, even light on the face matters more than the background.
  • Pair it with the Passport Size Photo Maker if you also need the exact passport dimensions.
