Signature Resizer

Sign on white paper, take a photo — this tool crops it, cleans it up and shrinks it to the exact size your form asks for, usually under 10 KB or 20 KB.

Launching with the MVP build: this tool is in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly.

Common signature targets

Under 10KB Under 20KB 140 × 60 pixels Custom size…

How it will work

  1. Sign with a blue or black pen on plain white paper and photograph it in good light.
  2. Upload the photo, drag the crop box tightly around the signature, and pick your target KB.
  3. Download the clean, correctly sized signature file and upload it to your form.

Tips for a signature that gets accepted

  • Sign on unlined white paper — lines show through in scans.
  • Photograph straight from above, without shadows across the paper.
  • Make sure the signature in the file matches the one you use on documents — mismatches can fail verification later.
