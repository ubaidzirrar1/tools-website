Image to PDF

Forms often want your documents as one PDF — and under a size limit. Add your JPG or PNG images, arrange them in order, and download a single PDF, optionally compressed to stay under 200 KB.

Launching with the MVP build: this tool is in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly.

How it will work

  1. Add one or more images (degree, CNIC, certificates — whatever the form asks for).
  2. Drag them into the right order; choose page size and the optional “under 200KB” target.
  3. Download the finished PDF and upload it to your form.

Good to know

  • Photograph documents flat, in bright light, with all four corners visible — the PDF can only be as clear as the photos.
  • The whole conversion happens in your browser; your documents are never uploaded.
  • Need the individual images smaller first? Run them through Compress Image to KB.
