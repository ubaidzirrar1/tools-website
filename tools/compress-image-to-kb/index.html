Compress Image to KB

Shrink a photo to the exact file size your form demands. Pick a target, drop your image in, and download a file that comes in at — or just under — that exact size, at the best quality the limit allows.

Launching with the MVP build: this hero tool is first in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly. The page, presets and guides below describe exactly how it will work.

How it will work

  1. Drag & drop your JPG, PNG or WebP image (or tap to select it).
  2. Choose a preset target size, or type any exact KB value.
  3. See the original vs. final size side by side, then download the compressed file.

Why exact size matters

Application forms do not ask for “a smaller photo” — they set a hard limit such as “maximum 50 KB”. A generic compressor may hand you 73 KB or 210 KB and the form still rejects the upload. This tool adjusts quality (and, if needed, dimensions) step by step until the output lands at your exact target. And because everything runs in your browser, your photo is never uploaded anywhere.
