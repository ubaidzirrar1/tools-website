Passport-size photo for NEET (UG), prepared carefully

NEET (UG) applications ask for a recent passport-size photograph in JPG format, and the tool above is preset to the standard passport size: 35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI — exactly 413 × 531 pixels — with a maximum of 200 KB. Add your photo, position your face in the locked frame, and download a clean, correctly proportioned file.

One honest caution matters more here than any setting on this page: the National Testing Agency (NTA) publishes the photograph rules in the information bulletin for each year's exam, and the details have changed between years — the permitted size range (commonly 10–200 KB), whether the photograph must carry your name and the date it was taken printed at the bottom, and an additional larger “postcard size” photograph (4 × 6 inches) that recent bulletins have also required. This tool guarantees the pixels and the file size; it cannot read this year's bulletin for you. Download the current bulletin from the NTA website and check its photograph page before your closing date.

For the photograph itself, recent bulletins have consistently wanted the same basics: a recent colour photo, front-facing, ears visible, against a plain white background, with no cap or goggles. If your bulletin requires the name-and-date strip, our Add Name & Date on Photo tool prints it for you at the exact size — or size a studio-made file here, keeping the printed strip inside the crop frame.

Frequently asked questions

What size photo does NEET (UG) require?

A passport-size JPG photograph; recent NTA bulletins have commonly set the file size between 10 KB and 200 KB. This page's preset produces exactly 413 × 531 pixels (35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI) under 200 KB. The current year's information bulletin is the final authority — check it before applying.

Does NEET also need a postcard-size photo?

Recent bulletins have also asked for a larger postcard-size (4 × 6 inch) photograph, used at the admit-card stage. If your bulletin lists it, prepare the passport-size file here, and for the postcard version use the tool's Custom preset with the pixel size your bulletin states (at 300 DPI, 4 × 6 inches is 1200 × 1800 px).

My NEET photo must have my name and date printed on it. Can this tool add that?

Not on this page — but yes, with our dedicated Add Name & Date on Photo tool. It crops your photo and prints your name and the date of the photograph on a strip at the bottom, inside the exact total size and KB band, so no studio visit is needed for the strip. If you already have a studio-made photo with the strip printed, you can also size that file here, keeping the printed strip inside the crop frame.
