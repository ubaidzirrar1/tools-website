Photo & Signature Resizer for Online Forms

Almost every application form asks for two uploads — a photo and a signature — each with its own pixel size and KB limit. This tool prepares both in one place: pick your exam, and the exact specifications are applied for you.

Launching with the MVP build: this tool is in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly, with the presets listed below ready to use.

How it will work

  1. Choose your exam or organisation from the preset list (or enter custom pixels + KB).
  2. Add your photo, crop it with the guide frame, and download the finished file.
  3. Add a photo of your signature and download that too — both files, form-ready.

Typical specifications (always verify with the official notice)

As a guide: SSC and IBPS photos are commonly 200×230 pixels at 20–50 KB, with signatures around 140×60 pixels at 10–20 KB. Pakistani commissions such as FPSC and PPSC set their own limits per advertisement. Presets on this page will carry the current known values, and the Disclaimer explains why you should always cross-check the official instructions.
