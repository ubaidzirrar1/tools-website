Passport Size Photo Maker

Turn a phone selfie into a proper passport-size photo: correct dimensions, plain white background, and — if you need prints — a 4×6 sheet with multiple copies you can print anywhere for a few rupees.

Launching with the MVP build: this tool is in the Phase 2 build queue and will appear here shortly.

Sizes included

35 × 45 mm (PK / IN / EU) 2 × 2 inch (US) Print sheet — 4×6 inch Custom size…

How it will work

  1. Upload a clear, front-facing photo taken in good light.
  2. Align your face with the on-screen guide (head and shoulders inside the frame).
  3. The background is replaced with plain white; download the single photo, the under-limit KB version for online forms, or the print sheet.

Before you take the photo

  • Face a window or bright light — avoid shadows on one side of the face.
  • Keep a neutral expression, eyes open, no sunglasses or cap.
  • Any background is fine — the tool replaces it with white.
